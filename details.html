ENCANTO

12+ • 2021 • 1h 42m • Fantasy, Musical, Animation

The Madrigals are an extraordinary family who live hidden in the mountains of Colombia in a charmed place called the Encanto. The magic of the Encanto has blessed every child in the family with a unique gift.

Sobre esta película

Mirabel Madrigal, the only member of her family without magical powers, discovers that the magic surrounding their home is in danger. She might be her family's last hope to save their extraordinary gift. Featuring original songs by Lin-Manuel Miranda, Encanto is a celebration of family and self-discovery.
